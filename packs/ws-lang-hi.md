# Word-Smith: हिन्दी language pack

Add words under any heading, comma separated or one per line. Keep the English name in each heading's brackets. Hindi writes its postpositions as words of their own (घर में, राम ने), so they are listed under Prepositions. A verb is listed as its infinitive (देखना) and its forms (देखता, देखा, देखकर, देखेगा) are found from it by the Inflections; a verb whose stem is one or two letters long (कर, जा, आ, दे, ले) is too short for that, and its forms are listed whole. Vowel signs, the virama, the nukta, the anusvara and the chandrabindu are read inside the word.
