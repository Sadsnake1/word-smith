Hinglish (Hindi written in Latin script, often mixed with English) writes its postpositions as words of their own (ghar mein, ram ne), so they are listed under Prepositions. A verb is listed as its infinitive (dekhna) and its forms (dekhta, dekha, dekhkar, dekhega) are found from it by the Inflections; a verb whose stem is one or two letters long (kar, ja, aa, de, le) is too short for that, and its forms are listed whole.
