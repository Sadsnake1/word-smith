# Word-Smith: English language pack

English is built into Word-Smith; this note shows the words it knows, the UK’s spellings among them. Add a word under a heading, or move one to another, and it wins over the built-in; take a row out of Misused and it is no longer flagged. Read from here: the word classes, filler words, soft filler and Commonly misused. English’s own rules (its context, the passive, the reading grade) are built in and not read from here, and its quote marks, US or UK, are set in Settings → Text → Typography.
