# Third-party notices

## Tatoeba

The learned parts of the language packs (their Class pairs, Ending shares, Word shares and Rules) were learned from
sentences of the [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org) project, which we tagged by hand for Word-Smith. The packs carry counts and
rules learned from those sentences, not the sentences themselves.

Tatoeba's sentences are published under [CC BY 2.0 FR](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/), some under
[CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The per-language exports of 2026-10-11 were used, from
https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/per_language/. Thanks to everyone who writes and translates sentences there.

The accuracy figures on the [language packs page](docs/language-packs.md) were measured on the Universal Dependencies test
sets, which are used only to measure and never to learn from.
